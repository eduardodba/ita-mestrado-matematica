# Questões reais da prova de inglês (2022–2026)

Questões reais das 9 provas, com gabarito quando divulgado oficialmente. As questões de **gramática**
(Parte 2) estão transcritas na íntegra — são as mais "treináveis". Para as de **leitura** (Parte 1),
indicamos o artigo e o tipo de pergunta; o enunciado completo está em `../provas/ingles/`.

## PARTE 2 — Gramática (transcrição completa)

### 2025/1 — Q7 (negative inversion) → **B**
Identify the sentence that contains an example of negative inversion:
A) Rarely I have seen such talent. · B) Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
C) She hardly works on weekends. · D) No longer he lives here.

### 2025/1 — Q8 (uncountable) → **C**
"…we don't stock ______ you need."
A) a equipment · B) the equipments · C) the equipment · D) both B and C

### 2025/1 — Q9 (future perfect) → **A**
Identify the alternative that correctly uses the future perfect verb tense.
A) They will have left by the time you arrive. · B) By next year, she will complete her degree.
C) He is going to start his new job tomorrow. · D) We will be waiting for you at the station.

### 2025/1 — Q10 (past perfect continuous) → **D**
"I ______ the races for years before I finally won!"
A) had entered · B) entered · C) was entering · D) had been entering

### 2024/1 — Q7 (causative) → **D**

· D) get the windows cleaned

### 2024/1 — Q8 (passive + used to) → **B**

### 2024/1 — Q9 (future continuous/perfect) → **C**
Don't call at 10 because we _______ dinner.
A) 'll have had · B) 'll have dinner · C) 'll be having · D) 'll having

### 2024/1 — Q10 (position of "never") → **B**

agreement, so we haven't bought the ranch. · C) ...never, so... · D) ...never.

### 2023/2 — Q1 (neither) → **C**
Which phrase uses 'neither' correctly?
A) Neither wallet are mine. · B) Neither of these wallets aren't mine. · C) Neither of these wallets
is mine. · D) There is no correct answer.

### 2023/2 — Q2 (past perfect continuous) → **C**
"In 1982, I started studying Portuguese. In 1984, I visited Brazil for the first time."
A) I have been studying... · B) Before I first visited Brazil, I have been studying... · C) I had been
studying Portuguese for two years before I first visited Brazil. · D) Both B and C.

### 2023/2 — Q3 (reported passive) → **D**
The prime minister _______ his resignation tomorrow.
A) is expected to have announced · B) is expected that he will · C) is expected to will announce
· D) is expected to announce

### 2023/2 — Q4 (reported passive + perfect) → **B**
Hundreds of people _______ killed in the earthquake.
A) are reported that they were · B) are reported to have been · C) are reported that they are
· D) are reported to be
