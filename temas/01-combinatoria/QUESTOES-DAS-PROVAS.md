# Questões reais das provas — Combinatória e Contagem

Questões de combinatória/contagem extraídas das provas PG-EIA/ITA (2021–2026), no enunciado original (inglês).
Gabarito ao fim de cada questão quando oficialmente divulgado (2023 e 2026).
As resoluções comentadas estão no `README.md` deste tema.

### 2026 — Q5
A cube should have two of its faces painted in red, while the other ones should be painted in other
four different colors. Two painting schemes are considered equal if one can get all faces of the same
colors in the same direction by simple rotations. How many different painting schemes can be formed
for this cube?
(a) 15 · (b) 16 · (c) 20 · (d) 24 · (e) 30
**Gabarito: (a)**

### 2024 — Q1
A polyhedron is composed of 6 equal isosceles trapezoids and 2 equal equilateral triangles. Two coloring
schemes are equal if a composition of 3D rotations (no reflection) makes all sides with equal colors
coincide. Three out of the six isosceles trapezoids should be painted in black. How many different
coloring schemes can be performed?
(a) 3 · (b) 4 · (c) 5 · (d) 6 · (e) 9
