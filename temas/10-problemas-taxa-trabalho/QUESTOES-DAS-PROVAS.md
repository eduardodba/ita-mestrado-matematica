### 2026 — Q2
An upper water reservoir may be supplied by any of three pumps. Separately: Pump A takes 1 h, Pump B
takes 1.5 h, Pump C takes 3 h to fill it. If the reservoir is empty and the three pumps work
simultaneously, how many minutes to fill it?
(a) 15 · (b) 20 · (c) 30 · (d) 45 · (e) 60  [minutes]
**Gabarito: (c)**

### 2023 — Q6
Andy and Robert together swept a 280 m² floor in four hours. Another day, Robert alone took seven
hours for the same task. If Andy sweeps alone an area of 240 m², how long does he take?
(a) 6 h · (b) 6.5 h · (c) 7 h · (d) 7.5 h · (e) 8 h
